FAQ

You may be wondering…

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different: after a first chat, I send you a free, detailed quote with no surprises.

Do I need to pay a subscription?

No. My websites are static: they can be hosted for free or for a few euros. The only running cost is your domain name (around €10–15 a year).

How long does it take to build my website?

Usually a few weeks. The timeline mostly depends on getting the content ready (text, photos, menu, prices), which I can help you with.

Why an English or bilingual website?

On the coast, part of the clientele comes from abroad, especially in season. An English version reassures these visitors and helps them book or call you. And if your clients are international, an English-only site may be all you need.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes: I’ll show you how to update everyday information (opening hours, menu, prices). If you prefer, I can take care of it for you.
