Hi, I’m Emma.

FAQ

Good questions

Do we meet online or in person?

Most sessions take place online by video. If you live near Saint-Nazaire or Nantes, we can also meet in person.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching focuses on your present and future goals and on taking action. If therapy would serve you better, I’ll tell you honestly.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Yes. Many clients use a training or development budget. I can provide a formal quote and invoice your company directly.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule for free up to 24 hours before a session. Later changes count as a completed session, except in emergencies.
